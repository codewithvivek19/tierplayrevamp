# ADR 015 — Fracture passage and product invitation

Date: 2026-09-27

The user rejected the circular hero portal and requested a different transition, then asked to rebuild “Bring the next tier to your floor.” This authorizes the implementation below despite the older audit-only assignment.

## Direction

The supplied first reference informs the dark negative space, suspended stone and vertical scale. Lusion (https://lusion.co/) was inspected for the separation between expressive 3D artwork and restrained editorial content. These are composition references, not copied assets or a claim to reproduce their animation.

The circular hero disc and orbit rings are removed. A narrow moving light fracture sits between irregular mineral shards. A foreground stone crosses the camera during the existing scroll interval, creating an occlusion transition as the shards assemble into the destination architecture. The camera follows a continuous forward arc. Destination architectural rings remain part of the second reference scene.

The scene retains one canvas, one camera owner, the shared GSAP progress, pause/skip/audio controls and existing static/reduced-motion paths. Stone normals are calculated from the fractured geometry and interpolated to the assembled normals with the geometry. This avoids lighting fractured surfaces as smooth boxes.

## Interface

The closing section becomes “Make room for Tierplay.” with recovered Altitude/Pinnacle product images, cabinet comparison and a direct sales link. The repetitive homepage contact band is removed. Generic claims, pill labels, moving ticker decoration and redundant surface effects are reduced; essential navigation and the legacy market restriction remain.

## Limits

The WebGL environment is procedural art, not a photo-exact reconstruction. Static fallback still uses the supplied reference artwork. Approved cabinet GLBs remain unavailable. No physical-device GPU or Safari/Firefox verification is claimed.
