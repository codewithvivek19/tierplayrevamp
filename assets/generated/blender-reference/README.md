## Source fidelity

The recovered public front-view cabinet images are authoritative for the visible silhouette and controls. Side and rear construction is a conservative generated proposal because approved CAD, manufacturer elevations and rear photography are unavailable. Do not use the proposed concealed geometry for manufacturing.

The V2 logo editions composite the exact official Tierplay SVG after generation. This avoids misspelled or approximate AI-rendered branding.

## Generation direction

The prompts requested orthographic industrial product visualization with flat studio lighting, physically based charcoal materials, restrained Tierplay lighting, consistent dimensions across every view and no labels, annotations, environmental scenery or dramatic lens distortion.
